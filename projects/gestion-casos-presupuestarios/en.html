Budget Case Management System

A full system to track school budget audits, from start to finish.

Problem

The accounting firm A&M Consultores was tracking school budget audits manually and across scattered sources, making it hard to see the status of each case and its supporting evidence at a glance.

Solution

A full system built in C# / .NET, in collaboration with Kelvin García, to centralize the tracking of school budget audits from start to finish: case registration, status control, and access to the relevant information for each audit from a single dashboard.

Stack & Architecture

  • C# / .NET with Entity Framework and LINQ for data access.
  • SQL Server as the database engine.
  • Layered architecture, separating business logic from data access and presentation.

Challenges

Modeling the full lifecycle of a budget audit — with its different statuses and stakeholders — inside a maintainable layered architecture, and coordinating team development with Kelvin García on a shared codebase.
